## 2. Roadmap

One long playthrough with no difficulty choices. Work through the locations, play with everything you see, and finish with a cleanup pass on the collections. Because nothing is known yet about revisiting locations, the roadmap front-loads a quick check and treats the open-ended trophies as "get them when you see the chance."

**Step 0 — First-hour check (no trophies)**
After finishing the first location, find out whether you can go back to earlier locations (free roam, a map, or level select) and whether the scrapbook of a finished location stays open. 
- **If you can revisit:** nothing below is missable; the order of Steps 2–5 is flexible.
- **If you cannot:** treat Step 3 and Step 5 trophies as **missable per location**. Before leaving a location, finish its scrapbook, try every activity you saw, and look around for benches, boxes and anything that looks like a hiding spot.

**Step 1 — Camera, scrapbook and dress-up basics (opening area / the Park)**
Take photos, place a sticker in the scrapbook, retake one photo to replace it, and visit the dress-up box. All of these are core mechanics you will touch naturally.
- Chikit! (Bronze)
- My Name Is Sparkleshot (Bronze)
- Oooh, Stickers! (Bronze)
- I Can Do Another One (Bronze)
- We Were at the Dress-Up Box (Silver)

**Step 2 — Story progression: discover each location**
Each location has a scrapbook that needs roughly 15 filled entries before the next one opens, so these unlock as you progress. The list order probably matches the game order `[inferred]`.
- We'll See... (Silver)
- Lucky's Dad's Rules! (Silver)
- Bye, Uncle Strawberry (Silver)
- Are These All for Free? (Silver)
- We'll Have to Live in the Forest! (Silver)

**Step 3 — Activity trophies: get them on sight, in whatever location they appear**
**⚠️ Possible missables while you play (see Missables section).** None of these is confirmed missable, but if Step 0 shows you cannot revisit locations, do them before leaving each area. Keep your eyes open for boxes, benches, hiding spots and drawing pages. Try to fill a second scrapbook slot with the same shot whenever two requirements look alike.
- Double Blueys Forever! (Gold)
- Am I a Good Drawer? (Silver)
- Did That Box Move? (Silver)
- 🙈 Hidden trophy #13 (Gold) <details><summary>reveal</summary><b>Found You!</b> — Take a secret photo. Probably a photo of something concealed in a location; unknown whether it exists in every location or only once `[unverified]`.</details>
- 🙈 Hidden trophy #20 (Gold) <details><summary>reveal</summary><b>Ahh, So Relaxing!</b> — Sit on a bench. Probably possible in any location with a bench; try it the first time you see one `[inferred]`.</details>

**Step 4 — Finish the story and unlock every album**
- Is the Game Over? (Gold)
- Home! (Silver)

**Step 5 — Cleanup: collections**
Open each location's scrapbook and look for empty slots, uncoloured Play For Real Life pages, and unfound pencil case items. If anything is missing, go back and fill it.
- For Real Life! (Gold)
- Hooray! (Gold)
- Can I Get Dad? (Gold)

**Final — Platinum**
- I Congratulations Your Ribbon (Platinum) — unlocks automatically with the last trophy above.

*Check: Step 1 (5) + Step 2 (5) + Step 3 (5) + Step 4 (2) + Step 5 (3) + Platinum (1) = 21.* ✅
